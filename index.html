About Me

I'm a Marketing Operations Specialist who builds the systems businesses run on — websites, funnels, automation, and content infrastructure, mostly inside GoHighLevel, and mostly from scratch.

Right now I own marketing operations for a financial education firm operating under strict compliance guidelines. I built their website from the ground up and still maintain it. I've stood up full CRM and blog infrastructure where none existed, built lead-generation funnels that had to pass compliance review before going live, and written the SOPs the rest of the team now runs on.

Before that, I spent close to four years running email marketing campaigns for an audience of 50,000+ subscribers, managing 2-3 campaigns a week and pushing click-through rates from 18% to 28% through consistent testing and optimization.

What sets me apart from most people working in GoHighLevel: I'm not limited to the drag-and-drop builder. I write HTML and CSS by hand and deploy it directly into GHL through Custom Code, which means I can build things most GHL users simply can't. If a client needs something outside the template library, I don't tell them it's not possible, I build it.

I also work daily with AI tools, Claude, ChatGPT, and Perplexity, as part of how I actually get work done: drafting and refining content, researching and fact-checking faster, building documentation, and automating pieces of my own workflow. This isn't a buzzword on a resume, it's how I operate day to day, and it's part of why I can move quickly without sacrificing accuracy.

I care about systems that outlast me. Every build I ship comes with documentation, so the people who come after me can run it without needing me in the room.
